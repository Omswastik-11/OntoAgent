# Escalation Matrix

## Overview
This matrix governs operational routing, approval authorities, and required escalations for non-standard e-commerce events.

## High-Value Orders
Any single order with an order value greater than R$500 means director approval is required before issuing goodwill compensation or manual adjustments.

## Large Batches
When the total compensation amount of a proposed batch exceeds R$2,000, operations managers cannot self-approve. Batch total > R$2,000 means director approval.

## Fraud and Security
Any transaction with suspected fraud, unusual address mutations, or repeated dispute filings goes to Trust & Safety for investigation.

## Legal Escalations
Any communication containing legal threats from customers or regulatory notices goes to Legal within 4 hours of receipt.
