# Electronics Category Handling

## Overview
Operational guidance for high-value and sensitive electronics products, including computers, smartphones, and gaming consoles.

## Shipping and Insurance
All electronics orders with total order value greater than R$500 MUST ship insured through authorized logistics partners. Standard unverified postal carriage is prohibited.

## Warranty and Defect Inquiries
Defects or functional failures arising outside initial transit damage fall under manufacturer warranty. Warranty claims go through the manufacturer or official brand service centers.

## Seller Eligibility
Given elevated dispute rates in high-value tech categories, electronics sellers with Probation status cannot join promotions or receive featured catalog placement.
