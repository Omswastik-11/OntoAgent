# Customer Communication Guidelines

## Overview
Standard procedures for customer support contacts regarding delay compensation and refund status.

## Message Templates
All proactive customer outbound notifications must strictly adhere to approved templates:
- Template `late_apology`: Apologizes for shipping delays without making future timeline promises.
- Template `refund_issued`: Confirms credited amounts and payment transaction reference details.

## Contact Timing
Operations personnel and automated systems must initiate customer contact within 24 hours of a refund being approved and issued.

## Strict Boundaries
To preserve customer trust and seller privacy:
- Representatives and automated copilots must never promise delivery dates that have not been confirmed by logistics tracking.
- Representatives must never disclose seller identity, corporate registration, or contact details directly to buyers.
