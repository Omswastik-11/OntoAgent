# Seller Service Level Agreement

## Overview
Marketplace sellers must adhere to strict fulfillment and dispatch deadlines to ensure a predictable buyer experience.

## Handoff Deadlines
The seller must hand over each item in an order to the carrier by the specified `shipping_limit_date`. Any handoff after this deadline is marked as a late handoff.

## Late-Handoff Rate Thresholds
Seller dispatch performance is measured over a trailing 30 days window, subject to a minimum volume of at least 20 items:
- A late-handoff rate greater than 15% triggers an operational SLA Warning.
- A late-handoff rate greater than 25% places the seller into Probation status.

## Probation and Suspension
Sellers placed on Probation status are subject to immediate restrictions:
- Two consecutive Probation months leads to a formal suspension review by marketplace operations.
- Sellers placed on probation are disqualified from participating in platform promotions.

## Response Timeframes
When notified of an SLA violation or inquiry by operations managers, sellers must respond to an ops notification within 48 hours.
