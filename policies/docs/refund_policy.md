# Refund and Compensation Policy v2026-01

## Overview
This document specifies standard operational compensation rules for customers experiencing shipping delays or delivery failures on the marketplace. All refunds require explicit review and approval by an authorized operations manager (`ops_manager`).

## Tier LATE_7
For orders delivered between 7 and 14 days after the estimated delivery date:
- Eligible customers are entitled to a refund of 100% of freight charges.
- The per-order goodwill cap of R$150 applies.
- Subject to the per-order goodwill cap and maximum payment received.

## Tier LATE_15
For orders delivered 15 or more days after the estimated delivery date:
- Eligible customers are entitled to a refund of 100% of freight charges plus 20% of item price.
- The per-order goodwill cap of R$150 applies.
- Subject to the per-order goodwill cap and maximum payment received.

## Goodwill Cap
The maximum goodwill compensation for any single order under delivery delay tiers is capped at R$150. Compensation cannot exceed total payments collected for the order.

## Undelivered Orders
For orders that are not delivered and are 10 or more days past the estimated delivery date as of the evaluation date:
- Eligible for a full refund of total order value (items plus freight).
- The goodwill cap of R$150 is not applicable to undelivered orders.

## Batch Limits
Automated and semi-automated refund batches are strictly limited to:
- A maximum of 25 orders per batch.
- A maximum of R$2,000 total compensation per approved batch.
- Batches exceeding either limit must be truncated or submitted to senior management for review.

## Escalation Thresholds
Any order with an order value greater than R$500 requires director approval and cannot be processed in routine operations batches.

## Ineligibility
The following orders are strictly ineligible for refund processing:
- Canceled orders.
- Orders that already hold an open or previously issued refund ticket.
